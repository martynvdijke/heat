2. Two kinds of account

HEAT has two separate identity models. They are not linked to each other.

Admin accounts

The race director or administrator signs in with a username and password. We store the username, a bcrypt password hash (never the password itself), an optional recovery email address, an optional OpenID Connect subject and authentication method, and web-session records. A web session contains a random session identifier, the client IP address and an expiry time. Sessions last up to 24 hours.

Racer identities

Racers sign in passwordlessly with a one-time link sent to the email address on file. We store the racer name, optional profile picture, car name and colour, team, points, rank and position, one or more email addresses, upgrade and legend-ability ownership, achievements, and race results. If a racer links a Telegram chat, we store the chat identifier and the Telegram username and first name that Telegram supplies. A player session stores a random device token, an optional device name and last-seen timestamps. Lap, sector, turbo, heat-card and gear-shift records may also be stored while a race is running.

Public visibility. Racer names, profile pictures, car and team details, points, rankings, race results and round scores may be shown on public leaderboards, public race state and the public API, including while a race is live. A driver-share link created by an administrator exposes that racer's statistics to anyone who has the link. Do not use a real name or photograph that you do not want published.

3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the championship service (accounts, sign-in, race state, results, upgrades): performance of a contract with you, or steps taken at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Running and securing the instance (session binding, rate limits, abuse prevention, backups, diagnostics): legitimate interests of the operator in keeping the service available and safe (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Email sign-in links and Telegram linking: consent, requested by you when you ask for a link or send the Telegram /login command (Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by not using the feature and asking for deletion.
  • Optional notifications (email, Telegram, Gotify): consent or the legitimate interest in delivering race information you subscribed to.
  • Legal obligations: where the operator must keep or disclose records under applicable law (Article 6(1)(c)).

5. How long we keep it

  • Admin account: until you delete it. Password-reset tokens expire after 30 minutes and are single-use. Web sessions expire after 24 hours.
  • Racer identity: until you delete it. Email sign-in and Telegram link tokens expire after 15 minutes and are single-use.
  • Player sessions: until you sign out that device, delete the racer identity, or an administrator removes the session.
  • Race results and round scores: when a racer identity is deleted, historical results are kept for the integrity of the championship but detached from the identity and the name is replaced with "Deleted racer". Gameplay records tied to the identity (laps, sectors, turbo logs, heat cards, gear shifts, race events, upgrades, achievements, stats) are deleted.
  • Server logs: kept for the period configured in the instance's log settings, then rotated.
  • Backups: periodic database backups are retained according to the instance's backup settings (seven by default) and then pruned. Deleted data can therefore remain in an existing backup until that backup is rotated out. Backups are used only for disaster recovery.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. The account pages provide self-service export and deletion for both account types. You can also use the contact above.

If you believe the processing is unlawful, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country. For a controller established in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; otherwise use your local EU/EEA supervisory authority. You also have the right to an effective judicial remedy.

7. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16. The operator does not knowingly create accounts for, or collect personal data from, children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided personal data, contact the operator so it can be removed.

8. Automated decisions and changes

Statistics such as ELO ratings, standings and streaks are descriptive calculations. They do not produce legal effects and are not used for automated decision-making about you.
